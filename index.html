    How it works

    From one press to help on the way

    1. 1

      Trigger

      Hold the button for 2 seconds, say your phrase, or let it detect a fall or snatch.

    2. 2

      10-second countdown

      The siren sounds and the app pops up. Your password cancels a false alarm.

    3. 3

      SOS sent

      Your contacts and the control room get your live location every 15 seconds.

    4. 4

      You’re safe

      Enter your password and everyone gets an “I’m safe now” message.

    Built for real danger

    Smart rules that protect you

    Only the app cancels

    An attacker can’t silence the keychain itself.

    Emergency password

    Looks like a normal cancel, but quietly keeps your SOS going.

    3 wrong tries

    Guessing your password sends the SOS straight away.

    No phone nearby

    The keychain sends an SOS beacon, and alerts go out once your phone is back.

    Fair misuse policy

    Cancelled alarms never count. Only repeated confirmed false alarms limit control room alerts.

    Private by design

    Your location is shared only during an alert or night tracking you turn on.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Do I need a subscription?

    No. The Basic model and the app are free to use. Only the planned Plus model, which has its own 4G connection, needs a small monthly data plan.

    Does it work without my phone?

    The siren always works. The Basic model also broadcasts an SOS beacon, and your alert goes out as soon as your phone is back in range. The Plus model will send alerts by itself.

    What if I press it by accident?

    You get 10 seconds to cancel with your password. Cancelled alarms are never sent and never count against you.

    What if someone forces me to cancel?

    Enter your emergency password instead. The app looks cancelled, but your SOS keeps going silently.

    Who is the control room?

    A 24/7 team that watches your live location during an alert. Details are being finalised before launch.

    Which phones are supported?

    The app is planned for both Android and iPhone.

    Is my location private?

    Your location is shared only during an alert, or while night tracking is on, and only with your chosen contacts and the control room.
